# 07 Two conventions for headings and intros

> Ignore these and both the contents and the library look bad.

## Convention one: line one is `# Title`

The site opens the lesson file and grabs the first line starting with `# `. If it finds none, it falls back to the lesson number.

So:

- line one **must** be `# ...`
- nothing before it — no blank lines, no comments, no YAML front matter
- one `#` per lesson; use `##` for sections

## Convention two: line two is `> Intro`

That intro shows up in three places: the lesson list in the library, the sidebar contents, and the top of the lesson.

The bar for a good intro: **after reading it, you know whether this lesson is worth your time.**

Weak:

```
> This lesson covers slicing
```

Strong:

```
> Slicing looks easy, but negative indices and step sizes are where people slip up. This lesson takes them apart one at a time.
```

## Separate them with a blank line

Title, then a blank line, then the intro, then another blank line. Without the blank lines Markdown glues them into one paragraph.

## Recap

- Line one `# Title`, nothing before it
- Line two `> Intro`, saying what this lesson solves
- Blank lines between
